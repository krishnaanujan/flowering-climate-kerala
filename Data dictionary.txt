Data dictionary for SWdata2014-2022.csv
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Date_of_observation |	Date on which the observation was made (can be added as a back date also)
Leaves_fresh	    |	"-2": "not observed/reported', "-1": 'don't know', "0": 'none', "1": 'few', "2": "many"

Leaves_old	    |	"-2": "not observed/reported', "-1": 'don't know', "0": 'none', "1": 'few', "2": "many" 
Flowers_bud	    |	"-2": "not observed/reported', "-1": 'don't know', "0": 'none', "1": 'few', "2": "many" 
Flowers_open	    |	"-2": "not observed/reported', "-1": 'don't know', "0": 'none', "1": 'few', "2": "many" 
Flowers_male	    |	"-2": "not observed/reported', "-1": 'don't know', "0": 'none', "1": 'few', "2": "many" 
Flowers_Female	    |	"-2": "not observed/reported', "-1": 'don't know', "0": 'none', "1": 'few', "2": "many" 
Fruits_unripe	    |	"-2": "not observed/reported', "-1": 'don't know', "0": 'none', "1": 'few', "2": "many"

Phenophase intensity Key	
-------------------------
None (0)	phenophase absent
Few  (1)        1/3rd of the canopy showed presence of  phenophase
Many (2)	>1/3rd of the canopy showed presence of phenophase
	
Notes	
-----

1. locations for some obervations may be missing. This occurs when observations have been made under poor internet connectivity	
2. Two types of observations can be made by contributors  - 'regular' (repeated on the same tree every week) and 'casual' (single incidental observation on a tree). The latter was introduced in 2018 and will reflect in the increased occurrence of single observation trees post 2018	
3. Data after 2014 are more consistent in terms of trees observed regularly, and observation protocol followed. 	
4. Phenopahses - Leaves_old and Fruits_open were introduced in 2018, and will appear as 'NA' before that. 
5. Phenophases - Flowers_male and Flowers_Female occur in only some species. For all practical purposes, these can be integrated with 'Flowers_open' phenophase
